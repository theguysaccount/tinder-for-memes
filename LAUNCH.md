## The first 1,000 users (machine plan)

Each completion produces at least one compare link; group chats multiply it (one link, several friends). If 40% of finishers share and each share brings 1.5 finishers, every 100 finishers bring 60 more. Measure with analytics once on (OPERATIONS.md key 4): completions, compare opens, `own_from_result`.
